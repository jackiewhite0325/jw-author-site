About J. White

J. White writes picture books for kids whose bodies and feelings work a little differently, and for the grown-ups who love them. The Muffin Series (26 books and counting) grew out of the real Muffin, a rescued pitbull with a masked face and a seizure disorder, who taught her that asking for help is brave and that a body which works differently is still a wonderful body.

A portion of every Muffin Series sale goes directly to support the ongoing medical missions of St. Jude Children's Research Hospital.

J. White is also the author of Don't Quote Me: Smart Mouths, Axolotl Dreams: A Coloring Journey, The Bingo Card of Chronic Illness, and The Many Faces of Grace.
