Welcome to the Healing Space

Welcome, however you found your way here. This corner of Sigil and Scribe is a space built for anyone: the parent looking for a story that finally makes sense of a hard diagnosis, the reader who needs a laugh on a rough day, or the person just passing through and curious what's inside. No matter your background, your body, or your story, there's a seat for you here.

Everything I create, the books, the memorial to Muffin, the free downloadables coming to this blog, is meant to do one of three things: bring a little awareness, offer a little comfort, or (when the day calls for it) just be plain entertaining. Some days you'll need all three at once, and that's alright too.

I'll use this space to share updates on new books, behind-the-scenes looks at where these stories come from, and free printables and resources as they're ready. Thank you for stepping in. I'm glad you're here.

J. White
