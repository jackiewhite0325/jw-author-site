A Series for Brave Hearts

Muffin the Pitbull Puppy is a 26-book series written to help kids understand and cope with chronic illness, inspired by a real dog who had seizures and taught her family what courage looks like. Five percent of net series royalties are donated quarterly to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital in her name. New books are added as they're published, right here.
