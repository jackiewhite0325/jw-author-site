The past doesn't stay buried. It stays knotted.

Introducing The Ties That Tear, the explosive first installment of the Trinity Tension Saga.

Follow Clara, a solo protagonist yanked from the modern day and forced to become a reluctant instrument of a cosmic balancing system. Her first assignment? The most famous marital disaster in history.

What to expect in Book 1:

  • A dual-timeline jump from modern day to Tudor England.
  • A deep dive into the psychological rot of Henry VIII's court.
  • A magic system where emotional trauma manifests as physical strings.
  • A high-stakes race against time that sets a 21-book saga in motion.
