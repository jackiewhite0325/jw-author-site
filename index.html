Brave comes in small, wiggly bodies.

The first question I get about the Muffin Series is almost always the same: why a pitbull?

Muffin was real. She was born into our family to Rosie, a rescued pitbull, and she was the runt of her litter. She struggled from the beginning, but she was ours and we were hers.

After she was spayed at six months, things changed. The vet suspected a liver shunt. We were saving for the testing when the seizures took over her days. From then on, most days, her small body shook with the wiggles, and all we could do was hold her through it and wait.

She was almost two when she passed. Almost two years old, and she taught us more about courage than most people do in a lifetime. She never felt sorry for herself. She got up, she wagged, she loved.

This series is how we keep her teaching. Twenty-six books are planned, in five phases: understanding her body, medical experiences, emotions, friendship and identity, and the big moments of a life. Each one is a gentle story built on a metaphor a child can hold, with coping tools and discussion prompts for the grown-ups who love them. They are educational storytelling, not medical advice. They are not a manual. They are a hand to hold.

A portion of every sale goes to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, in Muffin's name. A family that fights for a dog like her knows exactly what it means when someone fights for a child.

Thank you for being here at the start. Muffin Gets the Wiggles is in its final review, and this blog will follow everything from here: the making of the books, the homestead where they were written, and the small, wiggly truth that started it all.

You are loved, loved, loved.

About

J. White

J. White writes books for readers who need warmth, humor, and a little truth. The Muffin Series, a 26-book picture book journey, began as a memorial to her rescued pitbull Muffin, who taught her family that brave comes in small, wiggly bodies.

She also writes humor and survival guides for the chronic illness community, quote collections for the sharp-tongued, and coloring books for anyone who needs to slow down. J. White writes from a small homestead where the day starts with chickens, pigs, and a watering can, and ends with a manuscript open on the kitchen table. She publishes under her imprint, Sigil and Scribe, LLC.

The Muffin Series is her flagship work: a planned 26-book picture book series that helps young children understand their bodies, their medical experiences, and their big feelings. In the books, Muffin the character lives with the wiggles, a gentle name for seizures, and learns that her body works a little differently but she is still wonderful. Every book closes with the same promise: you are loved, loved, loved.

The real Muffin was a rescued pitbull, the runt of her litter, who lived with seizures for most of her short life. She was almost two when she passed. These books are how her family keeps her teaching: that asking for help is brave, and that a body that works a little differently is still a wonderful body.
A portion of Muffin Series royalties is donated quarterly to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, in memory of Muffin.
