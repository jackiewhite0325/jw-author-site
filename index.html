About

J. White

Author photo
(bookshelf portrait)

J. White writes books for readers who need warmth, humor, and a little truth. The Muffin Series, a 26-book picture book journey, began as a memorial to her rescued pitbull Muffin, who taught her family that brave comes in small, wiggly bodies.

She also writes humor and survival guides for the chronic illness community, quote collections for the sharp-tongued, and coloring books for anyone who needs to slow down. J. White writes from a small homestead where the day starts with chickens, pigs, and a watering can, and ends with a manuscript open on the kitchen table. She publishes under her imprint, Sigil and Scribe, LLC.

The Muffin Series is her flagship work: a planned 26-book picture book series that helps young children understand their bodies, their medical experiences, and their big feelings. In the books, Muffin the character lives with the wiggles, a gentle name for seizures, and learns that her body works a little differently but she is still wonderful. Every book closes with the same promise: you are loved, loved, loved.

The real Muffin was a rescued pitbull, the runt of her litter, who lived with seizures for most of her short life. She was almost two when she passed. These books are how her family keeps her teaching: that asking for help is brave, and that a body that works a little differently is still a wonderful body.
A portion of Muffin Series royalties is donated quarterly to St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital, in memory of Muffin.
